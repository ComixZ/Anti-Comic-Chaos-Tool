**Bring order to your comic, manga, and graphic novel collection – no installation, no subscription, no cloud required.**

Anti-Comic-Chaos-Tool is a lightweight, portable desktop application that keeps precise track of which comics, mangas, issues, and collected editions you already own. Whether it's 10 volumes or a collection with thousands of items: ACCT keeps the overview that a spreadsheet or your memory eventually can't handle anymore.

---

## ✨ Why ACCT?

- **🚀 No installation required** – one folder, one `.exe`. Just launch it and it works. Copied to a USB stick, your entire collection keeps running on any other PC too.
- **💸 Completely free, no ads, no tracking** – licensed under the PolyForm Noncommercial License, free to use without restriction for private use.
- **🔌 Works offline** – no registration, no cloud requirement, no internet connection needed (only the optional web search for pre-filling data is online).
- **📚 Built for the quirks of comic collections**, not generic inventory lists:
  - **Collected editions** – thick omnibus volumes cleanly linked to the individual issues they contain
  - **Anthologies** – multiple stories by different artists/writers within one issue, tracked individually
  - **Groups** – bundle series and spin-offs together, including custom images for slipcases, characters, and artwork
  - **Batch creation** – add ten, twenty, a hundred issues of a series with individual covers in one go instead of typing them in one by one
- **🖼️ Modern image formats** – covers in PNG, JPEG, WebP, and AVIF.
- **🧹 Self-cleaning collection** – built-in tools find orphaned cover files and duplicates (via image similarity detection, not just filename matching) and automatically repair missing thumbnails.
- **🔄 Import & export between databases** – move or copy comics between collections as needed, including automatic duplicate detection.
- **🔍 Fast to search & filter** – full-text search, color-coding by collection status, sortable series overview.
- **💾 Automatic backup** on program startup, optionally also at fixed intervals.

## 🖥️ Who is this for?

For anyone who wants to keep track of their physical (or digital) comic/manga/graphic novel collection without signing up for a subscription, uploading their data to someone else's cloud, or making do with collection software designed for stamps or books that doesn't understand the quirks of comics (collected editions, anthologies, issue numbering, variant covers).

---

## 📦 Getting started

1. Download the latest release
2. Unzip – `acct.exe`, `comic.ini`, and the corresponding `DB_*` folder must stay in the same directory
3. Launch – done

No admin rights, no installer, no registry entries.
